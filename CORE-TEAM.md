# Core Team

The core team contributes to the specification on a sustained basis — architecture, threat model, method, and conformance material — alongside the maintainers.

Core team membership is **not** authorship of a published version, and carries **no final decision authority**: RFC outcomes and merges remain with the maintainers during Public Review (`GOVERNANCE.md` §3). Members are invited by the maintainers on the basis of sustained, substantive contribution.

## Joining

There is no application process. The maintainers invite contributors whose work on the specification has been sustained and substantive. Active reviewers and pilot leads are natural candidates — see the [Public Review Program](public-review/README.md).

## Relationship to the other roles

- **Maintainers** ([`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md)) — final decision authority during Public Review.
- **Core team** (this file) — sustained specification work, no final authority.
- **Reviewers** ([`REVIEWERS.md`](REVIEWERS.md)) — recurring review of RFCs and findings.
- **Contributors** ([`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md)) — everyone else who improves the project.

All roles are defined in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) §3.
